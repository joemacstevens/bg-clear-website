What We Built

We've built the e-commerce portal we discussed. It has three experiences: one for your pharmacy customers, one for your sales reps, and one for you and Gina to manage everything. Customers can browse your DME catalog, request quotes, and track orders — but they never see pricing. Your reps see the full pricing schedule with BG Cost, Target, and Suggested prices, and can build quotes with built-in guardrails. You control the products, pricing rules, users, and approvals from the admin panel.

The Customer Experience

What your pharmacy customers see when they log in — clean, simple, pricing-free.

Login / Register
Customers register with their pharmacy name and contact info. Clean, branded sign-in experience.
Product Catalog
37 products across 4 categories with search and filters. No pricing shown — by design.
Product Detail
Customers see full specs, descriptions, and vendor info. They add items to their quote cart from here.
Quote Cart
They build a quote request and submit it. Your rep gets the request and can start pricing.
Quote History
Customers track all their quote requests and see status updates as your rep works them.
Order History
Full order tracking with status timeline — from placed to delivered.

The Rep Experience

What your sales reps see — full pricing visibility, quote building tools, and commission tracking.

Rep Dashboard
Reps see their assigned customers, pending quotes, active orders, and total commission at a glance.
Pricing Table
Every product with BG Cost, Target Price, Suggested Price, and Commission — auto-calculated from your schedule.
⭐ Quote Builder — The Core Pricing Workflow

Reps set prices within your guardrails. The system shows BG Cost, Target, and Suggested for each item. Commission updates in real-time as they adjust pricing.

🟢 Green
At or above Suggested
🟡 Amber
Between Target & Suggested
🔴 Red
Below Target — needs your approval
⛔ Dark Red
At/below BG Cost — no commission
Order Management
Reps track orders through the full lifecycle with status filters and commission visibility per order.

The Admin Experience

Your command center — manage products, pricing, users, and approvals. No developer needed.

Admin Dashboard
At-a-glance metrics: total products, active orders, pending approvals, and revenue. Plus quick links and activity feed.
Product Management
You and Gina can add, edit, and manage products directly. Full pricing visibility including vendor cost.
Pricing Rules
Your exact pricing schedule is built in. Change a percentage and every product recalculates automatically.
User Management
Assign reps to customers, change user roles, and manage your entire team from one place.
Approval Queue
When a rep prices below target, the order lands here for your approval before it moves forward.
