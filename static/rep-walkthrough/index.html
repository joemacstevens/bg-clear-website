How a Rep Builds an Order for a Customer

An end-to-end tour of the rep workflow — from signing in to closing an in-person order. Read top to bottom and follow along yourself.

What's in this guide

  1. Sign in & land on the Rep Dashboard
  2. Create a new customer
  3. Open the customer profile & start a quote
  4. Browse the catalog & add products
  5. Review the quote & pricing guardrails
  6. Close the order in person
  7. A couple of things to also know
STEP 1

Sign in & land on the Rep Dashboard

Go to bgclear.com/login and sign in with your rep credentials. The app drops you on your Rep Dashboard at /rep — your home base.

The dashboard shows your assigned customers, pending quotes, active orders, and total commission at a glance, plus the quotes that need your attention right now.

STEP 2

Create a new customer

Click Customers in the side nav to reach /rep/customers. Hit the navy + New Customer button in the top right to open the inline create form.

Your existing customers in a tidy card grid. The + New Customer button is top-right.

Heads-up: creating a customer sends a real welcome email from Resend. When you're testing, always use a throwaway address — not a real person's inbox.

Fill in the form. Only Full name and Email are required, but Company and Phone make the customer easier to recognize later. Click Create Account.

Filled-in form for our demo customer "Even Hostetler" at Hostetler Health Supply, using the safe throwaway address joemac+guidetest@ajeo.design.

You'll get a green confirmation banner with the new account number and a one-time temp password you can share with the customer.

Account created — note the BGC- account number and the temp password. The customer also gets a welcome email at the address you entered.

STEP 3

Open the customer profile & start a quote

Close the create panel and click your new customer's card from the list. Their profile shows contact info and any past quotes. Hit the yellow + Build a Quote button in the top-right.

Even's profile — no quotes yet. Click + Build a Quote to start one.

You're now in the Quote Builder at /rep/quotes/<id>. The customer's details are pinned to the top so you always know who you're building for.

Fresh, empty Quote Builder. You can add products one at a time from the dropdown, or jump into the full catalog (next step).

STEP 4

Browse the catalog & add products

For most quotes the fastest path is the visual catalog. Click 🛒 Browse full catalog → on the right side of the Add an item row.

Notice the navy sticky banner at the top: "You're building a quote for Hostetler Health Supply." That banner is how you know which customer's cart you're filling — it stays visible as you scroll.

Use the search box or a category to narrow things down, then click + Add to Quote on each product you want. Each card flips to a soft "✓ Added to quote" once it's in.

Three items added. The "Done — Back to Quote (3) →" button in the top-right shows the running count. Click it when you're done shopping.

STEP 5

Review the quote & pricing guardrails

Back in the Quote Builder, your line items show up in a table. Each row has BG Cost, Target, Suggested, and your editable Your Price. Prices default to the Suggested price — usually you can leave them alone.

Three line items, all at the Suggested price. Quote total $145.93, commission $21.26. The pricing guardrails (next paragraph) determine which button you get to close with.

Pricing color guardrails — what the Status column means:
At/above suggested green, good to go. Below suggested amber, still OK but the commission is smaller. Below target red, the order needs admin approval before it can ship.
You can tweak Qty or Your Price per line — the row recolors as you type, so you'll always see exactly where you stand.
STEP 6

Close the order in person

If you're sitting with the customer and ready to close right now, click the green Create Order Now button at the bottom of the Quote Builder. The quote flips into a real order — done.

Order page at /rep/orders/<id>. The order is stamped Approved, with a BGC- order number, the customer block, line items, and totals all in one view.

The customer gets an automatic order-confirmation email the moment the order is created — same Resend pipeline. Nothing extra to send.

A couple of things to also know

  • Below-target lines need approval. If any line is priced below Target (red), the green button instead reads "Create Order Now (Needs Approval)" and the order parks in a pending state until an admin signs off.
  • Don't have to close it yourself. Instead of Create Order Now, you can hit Send Quote to Customer and the customer reviews and accepts it from their own portal — useful when they want to talk it over before committing.
